ABSTRACT
========================================================================

The Triad Deployments — LavaLamp, Lazarus, and PharOS — are a
portfolio of digital identity resilience deployments derived from
the Possibilistic Security architectural framework (Green 2026,
v1.1) and grounded in the Closure framework's autopoietic-fixed-
point structure (Green 2026, Closure paper v1.4). This companion
piece provides the threat-landscape context in which the
deployments are positioned: a *cockroach / catapult / castle*
threat taxonomy combined with the *metabolic-value / predator-prey
ecology* axis that determines which threats are economically
justified for which adversaries.

The defensive architecture is the **castle / membrane / immune-
system** triad. LavaLamp is the substrate-level immune system that
constitutes the device's autopoietic identity. PharOS (forthcoming)
is the OS-layer membrane checkpoint. Lazarus is the inner-sanctum
backend with substance-under-the-hood / minimal-UI duality. The
defense-in-depth principle applies fractally: each tier has its
own immune system at the appropriate scale and resolution.

The honest security claim is **not** "we defeat all attackers." It
is "attacking *this* device costs more than the result is worth,
relative to easier targets." This is the cost-asymmetry framing of
LavaLamp's resolution-bounded security claim (LL-008), connected
explicitly to the predator-prey ecology of biological systems where
symbiosis — not annihilation — is the equilibrium.

This document covers (§1) the threat-landscape taxonomy
(cockroaches, catapults, castles, immune system, metabolic value),
(§2) where the Triad Deployments fit in the architecture, (§3) what
they DO and DON'T address with explicit scope discipline, (§4)
defense-in-depth via complementary systems at hardware, software,
operational, and strategic tiers, and (§5) honest framing of what
constitutes success — symbiosis, not victory.

1.1  The metaphor: cockroaches, catapults, castles, and the immune system
------------------------------------------------------------------------

1.  The framing for this paper:

      The best hackers are cockroaches and catapults (sometimes
      both). Cockroaches are small, persistent, hard to see and
      kill, you'll never kill all of them. Catapults break down
      the barriers set up to protect. Combined the two are lethal
      but rare. Catapults exist at a scale that is difficult for
      a cockroach to justify maintaining. Cockroaches exist at a
      scale that is difficult for a catapult to govern. Not
      impossible (maybe state-level actor). Our best defense is
      the castle, the membrane, with immune system constantly
      watching, waiting, digesting, incorporating, denying,
      obfuscating, repairing, etc at multiple scales and
      resolutions. We are alive and have identity. It is
      impossible for the enemy to provide a persistent attack to
      beat us without dominating in scale or injecting a
      virus/cancer under our nose.

2.  This is not a marketing metaphor. It is a *structurally
    faithful description* of the corpus's defensive principle:

    -  **Corpus-faithful.** The immune-system framing maps
       directly onto the autopoietic-closure structure articulated
       in the Closure paper's Q_51 / Q_102 autopoietic-fixed-
       point results. The immune system's "watching, waiting,
       digesting, incorporating, denying, obfuscating, repairing"
       is exactly the activity-pattern of an autopoietic system
       maintaining itself against perturbation.
    -  **Identity-constitutive.** "We are alive and have identity"
       is corpus-deep. Identity is *not* a static credential — it
       is the ongoing pattern of the immune system's activity.
       This is precisely LavaLamp's substrate-bound identity
       claim (LL-001): identity = the sustained chaotic
       trajectory, *not* a stored value. The trajectory IS the
       immune-system signature.
    -  **Defeat conditions named explicitly.** The only ways to
       beat a living, identity-coherent system are (a) **scale
       domination** (overwhelming the immune response) or (b)
       **virus/cancer injection** ("under our nose" — corruption
       from within at a level the immune system cannot recognise).
       Both are state-actor-tier in practice.

3.  The rest of this paper organises the threat landscape along
    these axes: cockroach-class, catapult-class, castle/membrane/
    immune-system architecture, and the metabolic-value/predator-
    prey ecology that determines which threats are economically
    justified for which adversaries.

1.2  Cockroach-class threats (small-scale, persistent, opportunistic)
------------------------------------------------------------------------

4.  **Profile.** Many small actors; persistent presence over time;
    adaptive but resource-limited per actor; survives by avoiding
    detection rather than overwhelming defenses. Cannot afford
    TEMPEST-grade equipment, multi-million-dollar zero-days, or
    hardware-interdiction supply chains. Lives in the gaps,
    seams, and operational-error spaces.

5.  Threat categories that cluster as cockroach-class:

    (a) **Operational / human error.** Phishing (credential leak);
        misconfigured deployment; user clicks malicious link;
        weak passwords reused across services; lost device. The
        cockroach's favourite habitat — the soft tissue between
        systems.
    (b) **Composability seams.** LavaLamp ↔ PharOS ↔ OS-auth ↔ MFA
        integration gaps. Each individual deployment is audited;
        the *interfaces between them* are where cockroaches
        squeeze through. The principle "attack the seams" applies
        at every scale.
    (c) **Long-term substrate drift.** Component aging;
        replacements (battery swap, RAM upgrade); slow-drift
        parameter changes that fall under the residue-audit
        threshold. The cockroach's patience advantage: drift
        victory over years rather than seconds.
    (d) **Active substrate manipulation (cockroach-tier).**
        Individual chip swap; locally-installed implant; physical
        keylogger. Requires brief physical access but no
        industrial supply chain.
    (e) **Slow-drift threshold gaming.** Opportunistic, patient
        attacks that evade per-instance detection by staying
        below the residue-audit threshold.
    (f) **Standard malware ecosystem.** Commercial spyware,
        ransomware, credential stealers. Deployed at scale by
        operators who individually can't afford catapults.

6.  **The Triad Deployments' coverage against cockroaches.** Good
    at the primitive tier. The residue audit (LL-006) catches
    slow-drift gaming via per-exponent vector residue rather than
    scalar KL divergence; the chaos-guard (LL-007) re-establishes
    entropy regularly so cockroach-pattern adaptation cannot
    slowly converge to a falsifiable pattern; LL-019 side-channel
    hardening closes the timing oracle that cockroaches would
    otherwise exploit.

7.  **Residual exposure to cockroaches.** The composability
    seams between LavaLamp, PharOS, OS-auth, and MFA are
    partially addressed by LL-023 (consumer-API surface) but the
    seams between the verifier API and the platform-shim
    implementation are where the runtime-conformance-vs-
    configuration gap (V-019) lives. Operational error at the
    user / device-administrator level is out of scope by design:
    the Triad Deployments give *device* identity, not *user*
    identity.

1.3  Catapult-class threats (large-scale, resourced, barrier-breaking)
------------------------------------------------------------------------

8.  **Profile.** Few actors per threat; high resource cost; can
    break specific defenses through sheer capability; rarely
    maintains long-term presence at the same scale (catapults are
    siege weapons — once the barrier breaks, the next move is
    occupation, which has different costs). State-level actors
    or extremely well-resourced criminal organisations.

9.  Threat categories that cluster as catapult-class:

    (a) **Passive substrate observation (TEMPEST / A7-class).**
        TEMPEST-grade RF / acoustic / optical / power-line
        emanation interception. Requires close proximity plus
        bespoke equipment plus ground-truth training data.
        State-actor capability tier; rare lower.
    (b) **Active substrate manipulation (catapult-tier).** Supply
        chain interdiction (modified hardware before delivery);
        chip decapping; bus probing in a lab; rowhammer-assisted
        exploitation; cold-boot RAM extraction at scale.
    (c) **Cryptographic primitive failure.** Quantum threat
        horizon; standards-body-driven algorithm migration;
        fundamental advances in algorithm cryptanalysis.
    (d) **Scale-domination operational attacks.** Distributed
        credential-stuffing; coordinated phishing campaigns
        against many users simultaneously; large-language-model-
        assisted social engineering.

10. **Coverage against catapults.** Partial at the primitive
    tier. LavaLamp's resolution-bounded security claim (LL-008)
    is honest about which adversary classes the bound applies to
    (A1-A6 with parametric capability levels, per LL-018);
    explicitly out of scope for kernel-level adversaries
    (LL-015) and tier-bounded for passive-emanation attacks
    (LL-025). The catapult-class threats partially covered (A4
    side-channel, A5 registration-time) require deployment-tier
    mitigations layered on the spec's primitive-tier defenses.

1.4  State-level threats: catapult plus cockroach combined
------------------------------------------------------------------------

11. The rare but lethal combination. State-level actors can
    simultaneously deploy catapult-tier resources against
    high-value targets and cockroach-tier presence for long-
    term reconnaissance. The combination is structurally
    difficult to govern because the cockroach scale is at odds
    with the catapult scale's bureaucratic discipline; state
    actors that successfully combine them (NSA TAO, PLA Unit
    61398-class) are rare and operate against specifically-
    valuable targets.

12. **Implication for deployment posture.** A LavaLamp-protected
    device holding state-secret content needs more than the
    primitive tier. The combination of:

    -  Hardware-tier complements (Faraday-rated enclosure;
       supply-chain integrity; TPM-sealed envelopes).
    -  Software-tier complements (reproducible builds; signed
       packages; SBOM transparency).
    -  Operational-tier complements (physical-access discipline;
       logging discipline; incident response).
    -  Strategic-tier complements (cordon sanitaire under ruin-
       class risk; jurisdictional placement).

    is required. The Triad Deployments form one tier of this
    layered defense, not the whole of it.

1.5  The castle / membrane / immune-system architecture
------------------------------------------------------------------------

13. The defensive frame from §1.1 maps onto the Triad Deployments
    component-by-component. Each component occupies a distinct
    architectural role:

14. **Castle — the perimeter / boundary.** The device's hardware
    itself plus the OS process isolation plus the cryptographic
    perimeter (TPM-sealed envelope, encrypted storage). LavaLamp's
    LL-022 articulates the castle walls' structural requirements:
    hardware root of trust (TPM 2.0 / Secure Enclave / TrustZone);
    OS sensor APIs at sufficient bandwidth; host-grade entropy;
    user/kernel process isolation.

15. **Membrane — regulated permeability.** *What* passes in,
    *what* passes out, *with discrimination*. PharOS (forthcoming)
    is the membrane checkpoint at the OS layer: it does not block
    everything; it filters via the LL-023 verifier API. The
    membrane is selectively open — that is the difference between
    it and a static barrier.

16. **Immune system — active processes at multiple scales.**

    -  **Watching.** The chaos-guard (LL-007) continuously
       estimates the largest Lyapunov exponent λ̂_1; the residue
       audit (LL-006) compares Lyapunov spectra against the
       registered envelope.
    -  **Waiting.** The WARMUP / VALID state machine (LL-007 +
       LL-012); only declaring readiness when conditions are
       actually met.
    -  **Digesting and incorporating.** The TRNG-driven reseed
       flow (LL-007) — the system absorbs fresh entropy and
       incorporates it into ongoing dynamics. This is the
       autopoietic-incorporation principle from the Closure paper
       operating at the SDE level.
    -  **Denying.** Per LL-017 no-oracle, the verification
       protocol denies threshold-probing surfaces.
    -  **Obfuscating.** Per LL-019 timing decorrelation, response-
       time distributions are statistically indistinguishable.
    -  **Repairing.** Chaos-guard reseed when λ̂_1 < τ_λ; re-
       registration on substrate change.
    -  **Multiple scales and resolutions.** Per-exponent vector
       residue test (LL-006); per-deployment N-scaling rule
       (LL-003); per-platform sensor strategy (LL-024).

17. **Alive and identity.** The immune system *is* the identity.
    There is no static credential to steal; the trajectory itself,
    sustained by the ongoing dynamics, is what the verifier
    matches against. LL-001 articulates this as the substrate-
    bound-identity primitive: identity is the verb, not the noun.

18. **The defeat conditions named in the metaphor:**

    (a) **Domination in scale.** The attacker overwhelms the
        immune response with so many simultaneous attacks that
        the defender cannot respond to all of them. Defended
        against by ensuring the immune system operates at
        *multiple scales and resolutions* — single-axis attacks
        are absorbed; only multi-axis state-level operations
        have a chance.
    (b) **Virus / cancer injection ("under our nose").** Corruption
        from within at a level the immune system cannot
        recognise. This is the **C-conjugate adversary** problem
        from Possibilistic Security — the attacker mimics the
        genuine system structurally, not just behaviourally. The
        Closure framework's 0/5202 cross-sector autopoiesis
        result says this is structurally impossible *under the
        autopoietic closure condition*; LL-006's residue audit
        gives the empirical-tier defense by detecting the
        structural mismatch in the Lyapunov spectrum.

19. The Triad Deployments are *the substrate-tier immune system*.
    They do not claim to defeat all attackers; they claim to make
    state-level attacks expensive AND detectable, and to position
    sub-state-level attackers outside their cost-justified zone.

1.6  Metabolic value and the predator-prey ecology
------------------------------------------------------------------------

20. The cockroach / catapult / castle metaphor describes *how* the
    defender works. It does not describe *why most attackers do
    not bother in the first place*. The complementary axis is
    **metabolic value / predator-prey ecology**.

21. The framing:

      The "metabolic value" of the target. If the defense is such
      that it is not worth the risk for the attacker (not enough
      value) they will go find something else. This is how we
      achieve symbiosis in the ecosystem. Lions and Gorillas...
      Hyenas and Lions... there are spats sure but they find
      their easy food to eat.

22. This is a different axis than scale (cockroach vs catapult)
    or defense (castle / membrane / immune system). It is the
    *attacker's cost-benefit calculation*. Every adversary has
    finite resources — time, money, attention, computational
    budget, operational risk-of-getting-caught. They prioritize
    targets by *expected value / expected cost*. They do not
    attack targets where the math does not favor it.

23. **The defender does not need to be invincible.** The defender
    needs to be either:

    -  **More expensive than nearby alternatives** (cost-asymmetry
       in the defender's favor), OR
    -  **Less valuable than nearby alternatives** (target-asymmetry
       in the defender's favor — be a less attractive prize).

    Either positions the target outside the attacker's cost-
    justified zone, and the attacker finds easier food elsewhere.

24. **Examples from biological ecology.**

    -  **Lions vs Gorillas.** Spats happen; mostly mutual
       respect. Gorillas are too costly to attack for marginal
       protein return; lions are too dangerous to attack for
       marginal territorial advantage. Both are large-bodied,
       well-defended; predation cost > predation benefit.
       Equilibrium: peaceful coexistence.
    -  **Hyenas vs Lions.** Spats over kills. Each occasionally
       steals from the other, but full conflict is rare; the cost
       of escalation exceeds the value of the disputed kill.
       Each species hunts its own ranges; they share an ecology
       without merging into one.
    -  **Wolves vs Bears.** Boundary conflicts at scarce resources
       (winter kills; salmon runs); mutual avoidance otherwise.

25. **The bacterial-immune-system parallel.** Most non-self
    bacteria are not attacked. The gut microbiome is mostly
    mutualistic; the immune system *digests and incorporates*
    what is useful. Adversarial response is reserved for
    pathogens that threaten the metabolic budget. Symbiosis is
    the default; adversarial response is the exception. The
    immune system is *not* trying to kill everything outside the
    self-boundary. It is trying to maintain *metabolic balance*
    — incorporating what is useful, denying what is harmful,
    mostly leaving everything else alone.

26. This is corpus-deep. The closure-of-self is *not* defined by
    killing all non-self; it is defined by maintaining the
    *metabolic conditions* under which the system persists. An
    identity-coherent system thrives in an ecology where most of
    the "non-self" is symbiotic, not adversarial.

1.7  Asymmetric cost as the security claim — symbiosis as equilibrium
------------------------------------------------------------------------

27. The metabolic-value framing reframes what LavaLamp's security
    claim *is*. The honest claim is **not** "we defeat all
    attackers." It is:

      **Attacking *this* device costs more than the result is
      worth, relative to easier targets.**

28. This is exactly what LavaLamp's LL-008 (resolution-bounded
    security claim) says formally. The resolution-bound is
    fundamentally about adversary measurement *cost* exceeding
    *value*: the adversary's measurement resolution is bounded by
    their budget; the device's chaos-production rate exceeds
    plausible adversary resolution at typical adversary cost
    levels.

29. LL-008 already captures the cost-asymmetry implicitly. The
    metabolic-value framing makes it *explicit* and connects it
    to the broader corpus:

    -  **Possibilistic Security** is about raising the cost of
       unsanctioned alternatives. Same shape: make the closure
       cheaper to honor than to break.
    -  **Cordon sanitaire under ruin-class risk** is structural
       cost-multiplication: the access cost is made prohibitive
       by structural disconnection rather than by stronger
       defense at the perimeter.
    -  **Asymmetry-trap watch** is the discipline of matching the
       *claim* to the *cost-asymmetry*. Don't claim invincibility;
       claim "expensive to defeat" with bounds.
    -  **Detection-postured rather than prevention-postured.**
       Accept that some attacks are economically justified for
       state actors; ensure they are *expensive AND detectable*,
       not *impossible*.

30. **Translation across the threat tiers:**

    | Tier                   | Cost-asymmetry strategy             |
    |------------------------|-------------------------------------|
    | Cockroach              | Modest defense exceeds their tiny   |
    |                        | budget; they go elsewhere. Trivial  |
    |                        | to position outside their cost-     |
    |                        | justified zone.                     |
    | Catapult               | Some attacks ARE economically       |
    |                        | justified for state actors. The     |
    |                        | defender's job: be more expensive   |
    |                        | than the cheapest catapult target.  |
    |                        | Don't be the lowest-hanging fruit   |
    |                        | at this tier.                       |
    | State-level            | Structural disconnection (cordon    |
    | (catapult + cockroach) | sanitaire) is the answer — make     |
    |                        | access prohibitive. Or: don't be    |
    |                        | the highest-value target in the     |
    |                        | room.                               |
    | Symbiotic /            | Most actors live here. Mutualistic- |
    | non-adversarial        | or-neutral relationships; no        |
    |                        | defense needed beyond standard      |
    |                        | hygiene.                            |

31. Most of the ecosystem — by population — is in the symbiotic
    tier. The Triad Deployments are not trying to defend against
    *everyone*; they are trying to defend against the small
    fraction of actors who *do* attempt attack, and they only
    need to defend at the cost level required to make those
    specific actors find easier food.

32. **Symbiosis is the equilibrium goal, not victory.** The
    defender's true job is to position the target so the
    attacker's cost-benefit math says "go elsewhere," then to
    *detect and respond* to the rare actors who attack anyway.
    The autopoietic-closure principle expressed at the predator-
    prey level: the closure is not "kill all enemies"; it is
    "maintain metabolic conditions under which most actors do
    not bother and we detect the rare ones who do."

33. **Practical implications for the Triad Deployments:**

    -  **High-value targets need *more* defense.** The attacker's
       cost budget grows with target value. A LavaLamp-protected
       device holding low-stakes content can rely on modest
       defense; a device holding state secrets needs more.
    -  **Low-value targets need *less* defense.** Cockroach-tier
       budgets alone will not justify attacks on uninteresting
       targets. The Triad Deployments are sufficient for most
       consumer use without additional layering.
    -  **Defender attractiveness matters as much as defenses.**
       Operational discipline — *don't draw attention; don't be
       the most valuable target in the room* — compounds defensive
       value at zero marginal cost.
    -  **Defense-in-depth = cost amplification, not perimeter
       strengthening.** Adding layers raises the attacker's total
       cost, even if no single layer is invincible. This is why
       LL-022 + LL-023 + LL-024 form a multi-layer commitment
       rather than a single load-bearing defense, and why the
       five joint-defense meta-claims (LL-030 through LL-034)
       formalise structurally cohesive defense triads explicitly.

34. The honest framing for the Triad Deployments: they raise the
    attacker's cost across multiple axes (substrate-binding,
    sensor authenticity, threshold calibration, side-channel
    hardening). Each axis individually is bounded; together they
    shift the cost-benefit math toward "find easier food
    elsewhere" for the vast majority of would-be attackers, and
    toward "expensive AND detectable" for the rare state-level
    actors.

35. The Triad Deployments form a closure-of-three at the

2.1  LavaLamp — the substrate-level immune system
------------------------------------------------------------------------

36. LavaLamp is the **substrate-level immune-system identity**.
    The chaotic SDE trajectory is the ongoing pattern of immune-
    system activity; the Lyapunov-spectrum residue audit is the
    mechanism by which the system *knows itself* and *recognises
    foreign*.

37. In the metaphor: LavaLamp is *what makes this castle yours*.
    Without LavaLamp, the castle is generic; with LavaLamp, the
    castle has identity that cannot be transferred to another
    castle without the substrate also transferring.

38. Relevant LL entries: LL-001 (substrate-bound identity), LL-002
    (visual/security decoupling), LL-003 (Lorenz-96 SDE choice),
    LL-006 (Lyapunov-spectrum residue audit), LL-007 (chaos-
    guard), LL-008 (resolution-bounded security claim), LL-018
    (per-class adversary quantification), LL-019 (side-channel
    hardening), LL-021 (worst-case-adversary bound — the only
    LL-NNN entry currently at `:proved`). The five joint-defense
    meta-claims (LL-030 through LL-034) formalise structurally
    cohesive triads of these entries; see the LavaLamp whitepaper
    §5 for the joint-defense template.

2.2  PharOS — membrane checkpoint at the OS layer
------------------------------------------------------------------------

39. PharOS is the **OS-layer authentication membrane** — the
    checkpoint that filters identity claims into and out of the
    device's authentication subsystems. PharOS exposes LavaLamp's
    verifier API (LL-023) to the OS authentication
    infrastructure: PAM on Linux, Authorization Plug-in on macOS,
    Credential Provider on Windows. Each platform shim translates
    the verifier's accept / reject Bool into platform-native
    authentication results without leaking distance information
    to the threshold (LL-017 no-oracle preservation through the
    membrane).

40. In the metaphor: PharOS is the membrane gate. It does not
    block everything (the castle has to be useful); it filters
    selectively (the membrane is permeable to legitimate identity
    claims and impermeable to spoofed ones).

41. Relevant LL entries: LL-011 (registration ceremony), LL-012
    (cold-start window), LL-013 (cross-config transitions),
    LL-014 (threshold calibration), LL-017 (verification no-
    oracle), LL-023 (consumer-API surface). PharOS is currently
    forthcoming; the published whitepaper for it has not yet been
    released. The LL-023 surface in LavaLamp's spec carries the
    structural commitment that PharOS will instantiate.

2.3  Lazarus — backend / inner sanctum
------------------------------------------------------------------------

42. Lazarus is the **substantive backend** — the inner sanctum
    where the work actually happens. The biblical-resurrection
    naming reflects the substance-under-the-hood / minimal-UI
    duality: what the user sees is utilitarian; what does the
    work is alive and persistent.

43. In the metaphor: Lazarus is the keep at the centre of the
    castle. The membrane (PharOS) checks identity at the gate;
    the keep (Lazarus) is where the high-value work happens with
    the assurance that the membrane is doing its job.

44. Lazarus has its own repository at github.com/IridiumSoftware/
    lazarus. Cross-Triad documentation will land as PharOS
    matures and the umbrella becomes operationally meaningful.

2.4  The deployment-stack triple
------------------------------------------------------------------------

45. LL-022 (downward — what LavaLamp depends on from the OS) +
    LL-023 (upward — what consumers depend on LavaLamp for) +
    LL-024 (operational — real-sensor instantiation strategy)
    form the **deployment-stack triple**: a closure-of-three at
    the spec level that articulates the *operational
    preconditions* for the immune-system architecture to work.

46. In the metaphor: the deployment-stack triple specifies the
    *environmental assumptions* under which the castle's immune
    system functions. If those assumptions fail, the immune
    system cannot do its job — the castle is still standing but
    the alarm does not ring.

47. The deployment-stack triple is one of the five engine-
    discovered structurally-cohesive triads in LavaLamp's spec.
    The others (LL-016 + LL-024 + LL-029 sensor-defense,
    LL-007 + LL-019 + LL-022 reseed-oracle defense, LL-024 +
    LL-028 + LL-029 round-3 Tier 3, LL-006 + LL-023 + LL-029

    operational-deployment-stack) are formalised as joint-defense
    meta-claims LL-030 through LL-034. See the LavaLamp
    whitepaper §5 and THEOREMS.md theorems 18-35 for the
    structural composition shape.

3.  WHAT THE TRIAD DEPLOYMENTS DO AND DON'T ADDRESS

3.1  Within scope
------------------------------------------------------------------------

48. The Triad Deployments collectively address (at the primitive
    tier):

    | Threat class        | Coverage                              |
    |---------------------|---------------------------------------|
    | A1 remote software  | Strong (LL-006, LL-007, LL-018)       |
    | A2 unprivileged     | Strong (LL-006, LL-018)               |
    |   user-space        |                                       |
    | A3 kernel-level     | OOS by declaration (LL-015)           |
    | A4 software side-   | Partial (timing yes, sensor-          |
    |   channel           | manipulation partial via cross-       |
    |                     | validation; LL-016, LL-019)           |
    | A5 registration-    | Partial (TPM defends substitution;    |
    |   time insider      | LL-020 ε-DP defends observation)      |
    | A6 time-localized   | Partial (single-window observation    |
    |   observer          | bounded; LL-012 cold-start protocol)  |

49. Most of the strong-coverage cases are cockroach-class
    threats. The catapult-class threats partially covered (A4 /

3.3  Load-bearing assumptions
------------------------------------------------------------------------

52. For the Triad Deployments to be operationally meaningful,
    the following assumptions must hold:

    1. **Substrate not under physical interdiction.** No supply-
       chain compromise of the hardware before delivery.
    2. **Substrate not under passive electromagnetic observation
       above noise floor.** TEMPEST-class observation either not
       occurring or bounded by deployment context (Faraday /
       distance / EMI-filtered).
    3. **Software dependency graph integrity.** Build-tool and
       library dependencies are not tampered.
    4. **Cryptographic primitives function as specified.** TPM,
       PQC, RNG behave per their standards.
    5. **User is not compelled.** Substrate-bound identity does
       not survive legal / political coercion.
    6. **Composability seams are individually intact.** LavaLamp
       ↔ PharOS ↔ OS-auth ↔ MFA integration testing is performed
       and conformance verified.
    7. **Substrate is stable over the deployment's lifetime.**
       Or, if not, re-registration protocols are followed.
    8. **Quantum threat horizon has not arrived for adjacent
       crypto.** Or, if it has, PQC migration is complete for
       the specific algorithms the protocol layer touches.

53. These are *load-bearing assumptions*. Deployments at high
    threat tiers should treat each as an explicit operational
    requirement and verify it independently, not assume it.

========================================================================
4.  DEFENSE-IN-DEPTH: COMPLEMENTARY SYSTEMS
========================================================================

54. The Triad Deployments are *necessary but not sufficient* for
    high-assurance identity. Layered defenses are required at
    multiple tiers for the immune-system metaphor to work in
    practice.

4.1  Hardware-tier complements
------------------------------------------------------------------------

55. -  **Faraday-rated enclosure / TEMPEST-rated equipment** for
       passive-emanation defense.
    -  **EMI-filtered power conditioning** (consumer-grade for
       cockroach-tier; SCIF-grade for state-actor tier).
    -  **Shielded cables** (USB / HDMI / ethernet) for radiated-
       emission containment.
    -  **Supply-chain integrity:** trusted-vendor procurement;
       hardware bill-of-materials provenance; tamper-evident
       packaging.
    -  **TPM 2.0 / Secure Enclave / TrustZone** for hardware
       root-of-trust (per LL-022's required-mechanism enumeration).
    -  **Decommissioning protocols** for end-of-life (data-
       bearing components do not escape the security boundary).

4.2  Software-tier complements
------------------------------------------------------------------------

56. -  **Reproducible builds** so binaries match published source.
    -  **Signed packages plus sigstore-like infrastructure** for
       supply-chain provenance.
    -  **SBOM (Software Bill of Materials)** for dependency
       transparency.
    -  **Lockfile discipline** (already in place for Julia, Lean,
       and other ecosystems in the canonical repositories).
    -  **Static analysis** for known-vulnerability-pattern
       detection.
    -  **Privilege separation** — multiple OS users / containers /
       VMs depending on threat tier.

4.3  Operational-tier complements
------------------------------------------------------------------------

57. -  **MFA / phishing-resistant authentication** layered on top
       of LavaLamp's device identity (FIDO2; WebAuthn).
    -  **UEBA (User and Entity Behavior Analytics)** for cockroach-
       class detection at the user / device-pattern level.
    -  **Security training** for human-facing attack surfaces.
    -  **Incident response.** Assume compromise eventually happens;
       prepare to detect and recover rather than only prevent.
    -  **Logging and forensic-trail discipline** for post-incident
       analysis.

4.4  Strategic-tier complements
------------------------------------------------------------------------

58. -  **Cordon sanitaire under ruin-class risk:** structural
       disconnection with regulated membrane; resist taxonomy-
       greed; Taleb-asymmetric upside-vs-downside accounting.
    -  **Jurisdictional placement** considerations (legal-
       compulsion threat tier).
    -  **Cryptographic-primitive monitoring** (track NIST PQC
       standardisation, TPM advisory feeds, etc.); migration
       planning baked into deployment lifecycle.
    -  **Regime-awareness discipline.** Match threat-tier to
       defensive investment; don't over-defend low-tier
       deployments or under-defend high-tier ones.

59. **The defense-in-depth principle applies fractally.** Each
    tier has its own immune system at the appropriate scale and
    resolution. Hardware-tier complements provide the perimeter;
    software-tier complements provide the integrity guarantees;
    operational-tier complements provide the runtime monitoring;
    strategic-tier complements provide the macro-frame within
    which the rest operates. None of these tiers is sufficient
    alone; the Triad Deployments occupy the substrate-tier
    immune-system role within this fractal stack.

========================================================================
5.  SYMBIOSIS, NOT VICTORY
========================================================================

60. The honest summary of what the Triad Deployments are for:

    -  They are not invincible. They cannot defeat all attackers.
       That is the wrong claim shape.
    -  They raise the attacker's cost across multiple axes,
       formalised as five joint-defense meta-claims with machine-
       checked structural composition (LavaLamp THEOREMS.md
       §LL-030-LL-034).
    -  They make sub-state-level attacks economically unjustified
       relative to easier targets in the ecosystem.
    -  They make state-level attacks expensive AND detectable
       rather than impossible. Detection-postured, not
       prevention-postured.
    -  They depend on layered defenses at hardware, software,
       operational, and strategic tiers to provide high-assurance
       identity in adversarial deployments.
    -  They are sufficient at the primitive tier for most consumer
       use without additional layering, given operational-
       discipline hygiene.

61. **Symbiosis is the equilibrium goal.** The defender's job is
    to position the target so the attacker's cost-benefit math
    says "go elsewhere," then to detect and respond to the rare
    actors who attack anyway. The autopoietic-closure principle
    expressed at the predator-prey level: the closure is not
    "kill all enemies"; it is "maintain metabolic conditions
    under which most actors do not bother and we detect the
    rare ones who do."

62. The metaphor returns: we are alive and have identity. The
    castle has identity that cannot be transferred to another
    castle without the substrate also transferring. The membrane
    is selectively open. The immune system is constantly watching,
    waiting, digesting, incorporating, denying, obfuscating,
    repairing — at multiple scales and resolutions. It is
    impossible for the enemy to provide a persistent attack to
    beat us without dominating in scale or injecting a virus /
    cancer under our nose.

63. Both of those defeat conditions are state-actor-tier and rare.
    For everyone else — the population of would-be attackers from
    cockroach-tier opportunists to catapult-tier criminal
    organisations — the Triad Deployments shift the cost-benefit
    math toward "find easier food elsewhere," which is the same
    equilibrium the biological immune system maintains with its
    surrounding ecology.

64. That equilibrium is the goal. Not victory, not invincibility,
    not the elimination of all adversaries. **Symbiosis.** A
    federation of intelligences that mostly leave each other
    alone, with structural defenses at the substrate level for
    the rare cases where adversarial response is metabolically
    justified.
